Generator Troubleshooting Guide

Problem 1: Generator Won't Start
Symptoms: Unit does not turn on when switch activated
Cause: Electrical system needs reset
Solution: Turn off, wait 30 seconds, turn on again

Problem 2: Low Power Output
Symptoms: Generator producing less power than rated
Cause: Battery low or fuel issue
Solution: Check fuel tank, replace battery if needed

Problem 3: Overheating
Symptoms: Unit becomes hot, may shut down
Cause: Lack of ventilation
Solution: Ensure proper airflow around unit
